## Permitted Uses Without Prior Permission

- **Self-hosting your own instance**: You may deploy and run Locus (either unmodified or with your own code modifications on your own server) for your school, class, or organization while keeping the original name and branding, as long as you do not publish or distribute the modified version as a separate project or third-party service.
- **Forks for contributions and Pull Requests**: You may fork the repository to submit fixes, translations, and Pull Requests upstream without renaming or replacing the logo.
- **Factual reference, reviews, and attribution**: You may truthfully refer to Locus and use screenshots or promotional assets (`.github/assets/*`) in articles, documentation, tutorials, reviews, compatibility notes, and notices such as "Powered by Locus" or "Based on Locus", provided it does not imply official partnership or endorsement by the author.
- **Unmodified redistribution**: You may redistribute exact, unmodified copies of the repository or container images under the terms of the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## When You Must Rename and Rebrand (or Request Permission)

You remain free to modify, distribute, and even sell the underlying code under the [MIT License](LICENSE), but you must first remove the Locus name and branding (or contribute your changes upstream via a Pull Request) in the following cases:

- **Publishing a modified fork**: If you publish or distribute a modified version (fork) — including forks with translations, modified functionality, UI changes, or custom branding — outside of preparing a Pull Request for the upstream repository, you must choose a distinct name and replace the project's branding: logos and icons (`src/locus/static/icons/*`), the branding name/icon fields in `src/locus/static/manifest.json`, and "Locus" strings in page titles and UI templates (`src/locus/templates/base.html`, `src/locus/templates/auth/login.html`, and other page templates). You may still include a factual "Based on Locus" attribution.
- **Public or commercial services**: You may not use the "Locus" project designation or its logos as the primary brand for a public SaaS, paid service, or commercial product — nor in domain names or app store listings in a way that suggests an official or author-endorsed offering — without prior permission.
- **No implied endorsement**: You may not use the project's name or branding in any way that implies official affiliation with, sponsorship by, or endorsement from the Locus author (`1hebread`).
